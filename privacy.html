Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Creator Toolbox is a free, browser-based set of draft-writing tools for social media creators.

Information you enter

The topics, keywords, audience descriptions, and other text you enter into the tools are processed in your browser to create draft suggestions. The site does not currently send those entries to an AI service or store them on a Creator Toolbox server.

Saved work

If you choose Save, your drafts are stored in your browser's local storage on that device. They are not automatically sent to us or synchronized across devices. You can delete saved items within the site or clear this site's browser storage. You can also download your work.

Hosting and feedback

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information as part of providing the hosting service; please review GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

If you submit feedback through the project's GitHub Issues page, that feedback may be publicly visible. Do not include passwords, private account details, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

External links

Links to third-party services are governed by those services' own privacy policies. Creator Toolbox does not control their practices.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the site's features or data practices change. The date above will be revised when material changes are made.
